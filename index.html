About Payswift

Built for small businesses who run on trust — and need it tracked

Payswift is an offline payment tracking and reminder app that helps small businesses and individuals manage customer pending payments easily. Whether you run a shop, a service business, or simply lend and borrow among clients, Payswift keeps every rupee accounted for.

No internet connection is required to use the app — your entire customer ledger, payment history, and reminders are stored securely on your own device. Payswift is designed to be fast, simple, and distraction-free, so you can focus on your business instead of your books.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

Payswift is built offline-first. Customer names, phone numbers, payment amounts, and due dates are stored locally on your phone and are never uploaded to any server.

  • ✓ No customer data is transmitted over the internet.
  • ✓ Backups, if created, are saved locally on your device.
  • ✓ SMS reminders are sent directly from your phone's SIM — not through our servers.
  • ✓ The app may show ads (via Google AdMob) to keep the free version running; AdMob may collect limited device and advertising-identifier data as described in Google's own privacy policy.
  • ✓ You can request deletion of any locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the in-app reset option.
